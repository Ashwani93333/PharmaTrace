# 💊 PHARMATRACE
### AI + Blockchain Powered Drug Supply Chain Transparency System

---

## 🚀 Overview

**PHARMATRACE** is an intelligent platform designed to eliminate counterfeit medicines and bring full transparency to the pharmaceutical supply chain.

It leverages **QR-based tracking, blockchain immutability, and AI-driven anomaly detection** to ensure that every medicine is authentic, traceable, and safe for consumption.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

- Counterfeit medicines threaten patient safety
- Lack of transparency in drug supply chains
- No real-time verification for pharmacies or patients
- Data tampering risks in traditional systems

---

## 💡 Solution

PHARMATRACE provides a **secure and intelligent ecosystem** where:

- Every drug is assigned a **unique QR code**
- Each transaction is recorded on a **blockchain ledger**
- AI continuously monitors and detects **anomalies and fraud**
- Stakeholders receive **real-time alerts**

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🔍 QR-Based Drug Verification
- Scan QR code to verify authenticity
- Track complete lifecycle of medicine

### 🔐 Blockchain Ledger
- Immutable transaction records
- Ensures tamper-proof supply chain

### 🤖 AI Anomaly Detection
- Detect suspicious activities
- Risk scoring system (0–100)

### 🚨 Real-Time Alerts
- Notify stakeholders instantly
- Detect tampering or irregularities

### 🧾 Secure Transaction Logs
- Every step is recorded and verifiable

### 👥 Role-Based Access Control
- Admin
- Manufacturer
- Distributor
- Pharmacy
- Patient
